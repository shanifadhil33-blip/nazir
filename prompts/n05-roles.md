## Roles (one member may hold several; screens and the agent adapt to the combination)
- service_owner (UI label "Service owner"): approves rule pack versions and plans, approves BIG lessons, records the release decision, reads everything. Never registers or edits adapters. Never edits evidence (already immutable).
- supplier_maintainer ("Supplier maintainer"): registers and edits adapters, drafts rule pack versions (status draft or submitted only), creates agents and agent versions (kind baseline or repair; planted_fault versions are created only by the worker or an owner, for the labelled demo), proposes repairs (proposals). Never approves anything, never approves his own proposal even if he also holds another role, never sees HIDDEN split scenarios (to stop fixes tuned to the hidden set).
- quality_reviewer ("Service quality reviewer"): approves or rejects proposals (reason required, already enforced), overrides findings (approval with subject_type finding_override, then finding status overridden), approves scenarios, proposes and approves plans, confirms SMALL lessons and edits lessons. Never approves rule pack versions, never creates or edits agents, agent versions or adapters.
- viewer ("Viewer"): read only. Sees workspaces, members, member_roles, rule_packs, approved rule_pack_versions, agents, runs, findings, release approvals. Never sees conversation_messages, tool_events, state_readings, adapters, agent_versions, scenarios, scenario_meanings, plans, proposals, lessons, lesson_revisions, audit_events, and can never write anything or call nazir.append_audit.
- Fluid roles: permissions are the UNION of the member's roles, with one exception that always wins: nobody approves a proposal they proposed, and nobody approves a rule pack version, plan or scenario they authored (four eyes). Record the author: add nullable `created_by` member columns where missing (rule_pack_versions, scenarios, plans, agent_versions, adapters) in 0004, filled by a BEFORE INSERT trigger from app.member_id for nazir_app (caller cannot spoof it).
